Spruce Creek


For more information: info@casparschols.com

Spruce Creek will be a landscape hotel in the Blue Ridge Mountains Virginia scheduled to be completed in 2030. Among it's structures will be 50 rooms, suites and sleeping pods embedded in the landscape, a restaurant, a shop, a library and several folies and several art installions.
